# Test Noodle Bowl

## Broth

### Simmer

- 1000 g water
- 20 g dried mushrooms

Simmer for 20 minutes, then strain.

> **Checkpoint** — if the broth tastes flat, simmer 5 minutes more.

## Noodles

> **Choose** — one noodle.

### Wheat noodles

- 200 g wheat noodles

Boil for 4 minutes and drain.

### Rice noodles

- 200 g rice noodles

Soak in hot water for 10 minutes and drain.

> **Substitute** — glass noodles work too.

## Assembly

### Combine

- [broth](#broth)
- [noodles](#noodles)
- 2 scallions, sliced

Divide the noodles between bowls and ladle the broth over them.

> **Note** — serve at once.

> **Serving** — add chili oil.
