# Test Caramel

## Cook the sugar

- 200 g sugar
- 60 g water

Boil without stirring until deep amber.

> **Warning** — the sugar is very hot.

> **note** — a light-colored pan makes the color easier to judge.

> **Tip** — a wet pastry brush washes crystals off the sides.

> **Serving** — a pinch of salt on top.

> **Substitute** — corn syrup for some of the water.

> **Make ahead** — the caramel keeps a week in a jar.

> **Variation** — stir in cream at the end for a sauce.

> **Check** — the color darkens off the heat too.

> **Close** — keep the lid near in case of a flare.

> **Cheese** — a salty one stands up to it.
