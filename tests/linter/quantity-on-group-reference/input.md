# Test Tart

## Fillings

### Custard

#### Cook the custard

- 250 g milk
- 3 egg yolks

Whisk the yolks into the hot milk and cook until thick.

### Jam

#### Simmer the jam

- 200 g raspberries
- 100 g sugar

Simmer until it coats a spoon.

## Shell

### Bake the shell

- 200 g shortcrust pastry

Line a tart tin with the pastry and bake it blind.

## Assembly

### Fill

- [shell](#shell)
- 1/2 [fillings](#fillings)

Spread the jam in the shell and pour the custard over it.
