# Test Lemonade

## Make the syrup

- 200 g sugar
- 1 cup water

Heat the sugar and water until the sugar dissolves, then cool.

## Mix

- 1 1/2 cups lemon juice
- 2-3 cups cold water
- 2 large lemons, sliced

Stir the syrup, juice and water together and add the lemon slices.

> **Tip** — chill the glasses first.
