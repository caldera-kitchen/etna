# Roast Carrots

## Carrots

### Roast the carrots

- 500 g carrots

Roast until browned.

## Glaze (optional)

### Warm the glaze

- 2 tbsp honey

Warm the honey.

## Seeds — optinal

### Toast the seeds

- 30 g sesame seeds

Toast the seeds.

## Optionally chill the plates

Chill the plates.

## Serve

> **Optional** — a squeeze of lemon.

- [carrots](#carrots)

Serve.
