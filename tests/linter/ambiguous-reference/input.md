# Test Slaw

## Vegetables

### Mix

- 300 g cabbage, shredded
- 100 g carrot, grated

Toss the vegetables together.

## Dressing

### Mix

- 60 g mayonnaise
- 15 g vinegar

Whisk until smooth.

## Assembly

### Combine

- [vegetables](#vegetables)
- [dressing](#mix)

Fold the dressing through the vegetables.
