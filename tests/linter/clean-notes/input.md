# Test Shake

## Blend

- 300 g milk
- 1 banana

Blend until smooth.

## Serve

Pour into two glasses.

## Notes

- Keeps a day in the refrigerator; shake again before serving.

  Shake it in a jar with a lid.

- Frozen banana makes it thicker.
- Oat milk works too.
- **Mocha** — add a shot of espresso with the milk.

> **Tip** — Chill the glasses first.
