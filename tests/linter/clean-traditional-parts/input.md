# Test Beans

## Ingredients

- 1 bay leaf

### Beans

- 100 g kidney beans
- 1 tsp salt

### Sauce

- 10 g olive oil
- 100 g onion, diced

## Directions

1. Set out a pressure cooker.

### Beans

1. **Soak** — Soak the beans overnight.
2. Cook the beans until tender.

### Sauce

1. Fry the onion in the oil.
2. Blend until smooth.

#### Tip

Strain the sauce for a finer finish.

### Finish

1. Combine the beans, the sauce and the bay leaf.

## Notes

- **Storage** — Keeps three days.
