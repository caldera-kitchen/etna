# Test Soup

## Sweat the onions

- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons butter

Cook the onion in the butter over *medium-low* heat, stirring *now and then*, until *soft and translucent*, *8-10 minutes*, and *the butter has stopped foaming*.

## Simmer

- 1 liter stock

Add the stock and simmer on *low* until *thick*.
