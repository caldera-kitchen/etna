# Test Custard

## Cook

- 500 g milk
- 4 large egg yolks

Cook over low heat, stirring, until it coats a spoon.

> **checkpoint** — if it starts to curdle, take it off the heat and whisk hard.

> **Check point** — if it is too thin, cook 2 minutes more.

## Flavor

> **Chose** — one flavor.

### Vanilla

- 5 g vanilla extract

Stir in the vanilla.

### Coffee

- 3 g instant coffee

Stir in the coffee until dissolved.

> **Choice** — any instant coffee works.

## Chill

Chill for 4 hours.

> **Checkpoints** — if a skin has formed, whisk it back in.

> **Chekpoint** — if it has not set, chill 1 hour more.
