# Test Flatbread

## Dough

- 500 g flour
- 325 g water

Mix everything together.

### Knead

Knead for 10 minutes.

### Rest

Cover and rest for 1 hour.

## Baking

> **Note** — a callout on a component is allowed.

### Shape

Divide into 8 pieces and roll each one thin.

### Bake

Cook each piece in a dry pan for 2 minutes a side.
