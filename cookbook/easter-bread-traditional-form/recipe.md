# Easter Bread (Traditional Form)

## Ingredients

### Bread

- 20 g SAF Gold Instant Yeast
- 40 g water
- 8 g sugar
- 38 g butter, melted
- 150 g eggs — 3 large
- 5 g vanilla
- 130 g sugar
- 140 g whole milk, cold
- 567 g King Arthur all purpose
- 3 g salt
- oil — for greasing dough bowl
- oil — for plastic wrap

### Frosting — optional

- 90 g powdered sugar
- 1/2 tsp lemon oil
- water — for the icing
- sprinkles — for topping

## Directions

### Bread

1. Combine yeast, water, and 8 grams of sugar in a bowl. Whisk gently for 30 seconds to dissolve the yeast. Set aside for 15 minutes until the mixture is light and foamy. Gently melt the butter.
2. Whisk eggs and vanilla in a bowl. Whisk in the remaining sugar. Then add the cold milk and butter. Next whisk in the yeast mixture. Move liquid ingredients to mixer bowl. Add flour and salt to mixer bowl.
3. **Knead in a spiral mixer** — Spiral Mixer - Speed 2 for 4 minutes. 30 minute rest. Speed 6 for 6-8 minutes, checking dough temperature with a Thermapen. Stop when the dough begins to soften and bubbles can be heard popping under the hook, or when the dough reaches 85°F, whichever comes first.
4. **Knead in a KitchenAid** — Using dough hook, mix on low for 5 minutes. The dough will look very sticky and cling to the side of the mixer bowl. Increase speed one setting and knead for 5 more minutes. The dough will start to form a ball around the hook, but dough will still cling to the bowl. Stop the mixer. Scrape all the dough off the sides of the bowl. Turn the mixer back on to one setting above low and knead for 5 more minutes. In total, the dough will knead for 15 minutes.
5. Shape the dough into a ball, move to lightly oiled bowl, cover. Let dough rise at 78°F for about 90 minutes until doubled. On a clean counter, turn out the dough and press into a rough rectangle. Fold like a letter in thirds, turn 90 degrees and fold into thirds again. Return to oiled bowl and cover. Let dough rise again at 78°F for about 90 minutes until doubled.
6. Separate the dough into 3, 4, or 6 pieces depending on the braid style. Braid and place on parchment lined cookie sheet. Lightly oil plastic wrap and cover loaf. Let rise until puffy and dough does not spring back when lightly pressed, about another 90 minutes. While the loaf rises, preheat oven to 275°F with a rack one level up from the bottom.
7. Bake at 275°F for 50-70 minutes to 190°F internal temperature. Rotate after 25 minutes. Monitor the bread through baking and lightly tent with foil if it starts to brown too early. Cool completely on cooling rack.

### Frosting — optional

1. Combine powdered sugar, lemon oil, and just enough water to make a spreadable icing. Use a pastry brush to ice loaf and top with sprinkles.

## Notes

- 3297 calories per plain loaf, 3647 calories per iced loaf
- Finished loaf weighs about 1000 grams or 3.6 calories/gram of iced bread
- **Storage** — Store covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, otherwise slice and freeze.
