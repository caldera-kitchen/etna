# Italian Wedding Soup (Traditional Form)

## Ingredients

### Chicken Stock

- 1 large Rotisserie Chicken, separate meat from bones; reserve meat for soup
- 4 stalks Celery, cut into 2-inch chunks
- 4 large Carrots, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 large Yellow Onions, with skin, washed, quartered
- 4 cloves Garlic, with skin, washed, smashed
- 2 tsp Kosher Salt
- 10 Black peppercorns
- 12 cups Water

### Meatballs

- 6 oz Ground beef — 80/20
- 1 oz Fresh bread crumbs
- 1 large Egg
- 1 fl oz Whole Milk
- 1 1/2 oz Romano Cheese
- 3/4 oz Yellow Onion, grated
- 1/2 oz Garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp Dried Parsley
- 1/2 tsp Dried Basil

### Breads for Soup

- 2 large Eggs, beaten until foamy
- 2 tbsp Romano Cheese
- 2 tsp Dried Parsley
- 4 tbsp Dried Italian Bread Crumbs
- 1 pinch Black Pepper

### Wedding Soup

- 1 1/2 tbsp Better Than Bouillon Chicken
- 3 large carrots, sliced
- 3 large celery stalks, sliced
- 8 oz chicken, reserved from stock
- 8 oz escarole
- 3 oz pastina, orzo, stars, or acini di pepe
- Romano Cheese — optional, to taste

## Directions

### Chicken Stock

1. Break down chicken and reserve meat for the finished soup. Place bones, skin, and all juices in large stock pot with all other ingredients. Bring to boil and then reduce heat to simmer for 2 hours. Replenish water as needed. Strain to separate stock from solids. Return stock to the pot and discard solids.

### Meatballs

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Mix all ingredients and form small meatballs, each the size of a small cherry tomato. Bake on a cookie sheet for about 30 minutes, or until the meatballs are cooked through and well browned.

### Breads for Soup

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Combine all ingredients to form a soft batter. Lightly grease a small (9x11 inch) cookie sheet and spread the batter in a thin, even layer. Bake 12-15 minutes until golden brown. Cool and cut into cubes.

### Wedding Soup

1. To the strained stock, add bouillon, carrots, and celery. Bring to a boil and simmer 15 minutes. Add escarole, chicken meat, and meatballs. Continue cooking for 15 minutes. Add pastina and cook a final 15 minutes.

### Serve

1. Ladle soup into bowls making sure each bowl has a good balance of broth and ingredients. Add a few breads for soup on top and sprinkle with Romano cheese.

## Notes

- All ounce measurements refer to weight, as measured with a kitchen scale.
