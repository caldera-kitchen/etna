# Navy Bean Soup (Traditional Form)

## Ingredients

### Beans — the night before

- 500 g navy beans — or Great Northern, Cannellini, Flageolet, or 17 bean mix
- 3/4 tbsp kosher salt
- 1000 g water

### Soup

- 28 g olive oil
- 400 g ham, diced
- 170 g onion, diced
- 480 g carrots, diced
- 220 g celery stalks, sliced
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1 bay leaf
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 450 g white potatoes, peeled and diced
- 2000 g water
- water — to bring the soup to 3600 grams

## Directions

### Beans — the night before

1. **Rinse and brine the beans** — Check beans and rinse thoroughly. Brine in the salted water overnight.
2. **Drain the beans** — Drain and rinse beans well.

### Soup

1. **Sauté the ham** — Begin by sautéing ham in olive oil over medium heat.
2. **Add the vegetables** — While ham is cooking, dice onion and add to pot. Clean and dice carrots and add to pot. Clean and dice celery and add to pot.
3. **Season and sauté** — Add salt, black pepper and bay leaf. Sauté 5 minutes while cleaning and dicing potato.
4. **Add the garlic** — Mince and add garlic. Cook 1 minute.
5. **Add potatoes, beans, and water** — Add potatoes, and drained beans. Add 2000 g of water.
6. **Boil** — Slow boil for 135 minutes or bring to full pressure for 15 minutes.
7. **Adjust and serve** — Add water to bring total weight of soup to 3600 grams. Serve.
