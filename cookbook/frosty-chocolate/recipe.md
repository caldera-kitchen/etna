# Frosty Chocolate

## Cacao bloom

### Combine & rest

- 32 g water, at 110°F
- 8 g extra brute cacao

Combine and rest.

## Base

### Blend to 170°F

- 175 g sugar
- 20 g arrowroot
- 4 g lecithin powder
- 1 g kosher salt
- 375 g whole milk

Blend *in a Vitamix* *on high* to 170°F.

### Combine

- 375 g heavy cream

Combine.

### Cool to 110°F

Cool to 110°F.

### Add to Vitamix

- [cacao bloom](#cacao-bloom)
- 180 g milk chocolate

Add to the Vitamix.

### Wait 10 minutes

Wait 10 minutes.

### Blend 15 seconds

Blend for 15 seconds.

### Chill

Chill.

### Churn

Churn.
