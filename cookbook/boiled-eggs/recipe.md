# Boiled Eggs

## Boil the water

Bring 1-inch of water to boil in large pan with tight fitting lid.

## Add the eggs

- 1 large egg

Gently set eggs into boiling water and cover.

## Boil

> **Choose**

### Soft Boiled

#### Boil soft

Boil for *6 minutes*.

### Jammy

#### Boil jammy

Boil for *7 minutes*.

### Hard Boiled

#### Boil hard

Boil for *13 minutes*.

## Finish

> **Choose**

### Serve Warm

#### Dip and peel

Dip briefly in *cool water* to stop cook, peel, and serve warm.

### Refrigerate

#### Chill

Prepare ice bath. Immediate move eggs to *ice bath* and cool *15 minutes*. Refrigerate.

## Notes

- Typically soft and jammy eggs are served immediately, warm.
