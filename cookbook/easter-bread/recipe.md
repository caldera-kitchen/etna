# Easter Bread

## Bread

### Yeast mixture

#### Proof the yeast

- 20 g SAF Gold Instant Yeast
- 40 g water
- 8 g sugar

Combine yeast, water, and 8 grams of sugar in a bowl. Whisk gently for 30 seconds to dissolve the yeast. Set aside for *15 minutes* until the mixture is light and foamy.

### Melt the butter

- 38 g butter, melted

Gently melt the butter.

### Whisk the liquids

- 150 g eggs — 3 large
- 5 g vanilla
- 130 g sugar
- 140 g whole milk, cold

Whisk eggs and vanilla in a bowl. Whisk in the remaining sugar. Then add the cold milk and butter.

### Add the yeast mixture

- [yeast mixture](#yeast-mixture)

Next whisk in the yeast mixture. Move liquid ingredients to mixer bowl.

### Mix the dough

- 567 g King Arthur all purpose
- 3 g salt

Add flour and salt to mixer bowl.

### Knead

> **Choose**

#### Spiral Mixer

##### Knead in a spiral mixer

Spiral Mixer - *Speed 2 for 4 minutes*. *30 minute rest*. *Speed 6 for 6-8 minutes*, checking dough temperature with a Thermapen. Stop when the dough begins to soften and bubbles can be heard popping under the hook, or when the dough reaches 85°F, whichever comes first.

#### KitchenAid

##### Knead in a KitchenAid

Using *dough hook*, mix on low for 5 minutes. The dough will look very sticky and cling to the side of the mixer bowl. Increase speed one setting and knead for 5 more minutes. The dough will start to form a ball around the hook, but dough will still cling to the bowl. Stop the mixer. Scrape all the dough off the sides of the bowl. Turn the mixer back on to one setting above low and knead for 5 more minutes. In total, the dough will *knead for 15 minutes*.

### Place dough in bowl

- oil — for greasing dough bowl

Shape the dough into a ball, move to lightly oiled bowl, *cover*.

### First rise

Let dough rise *at 78°F* for *about 90 minutes* until doubled.

### Fold and rise again

On a clean counter, turn out the dough and press into a rough rectangle. Fold like a letter in thirds, turn 90 degrees and fold into thirds again. Return to oiled bowl and cover. Let dough rise again *at 78°F* for *about 90 minutes* until doubled.

### Braid

Separate the dough into 3, 4, or 6 pieces depending on the braid style. Braid and place on parchment lined cookie sheet.

### Cover the loaf

- oil — for plastic wrap

Lightly oil plastic wrap and cover loaf.

### Final rise

Let rise until puffy and dough does not spring back when lightly pressed, *about another 90 minutes*. While the loaf rises, *preheat oven to 275°F* with a rack one level up from the bottom.

### Bake

Bake *at 275°F* for *50-70 minutes* *to 190°F internal* temperature. Rotate after 25 minutes.

> **Checkpoint** — Monitor the bread through baking and lightly tent with foil if it starts to brown too early.

### Cool

Cool completely on cooling rack.

## Frosting — optional

### Make the icing

- 90 g powdered sugar
- 1/2 tsp lemon oil
- water — for the icing

Combine powdered sugar, lemon oil, and just enough water to make a spreadable icing.

### Ice the loaf

- [bread](#bread)
- sprinkles — for topping

Use a pastry brush to ice loaf and top with sprinkles.

## Notes

- 3297 calories per plain loaf, 3647 calories per iced loaf
- Finished loaf weighs about 1000 grams or 3.6 calories/gram of iced bread
- **Storage** — Store covered at room temperature for up to 2 days, otherwise slice and freeze.
