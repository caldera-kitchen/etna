# Deviled Eggs

## Peel and halve

- 1 recipe [Boiled Eggs](../boiled-eggs/) — hard boiled, refrigerated

Peel eggs. Halve each egg and scoop yolk into mixing bowl.

## Whisk the filling

- 1.25 g prepared mustard
- 14 g mayonnaise

Based on the number of eggs, add appropriate amount of prepared mustard and mayonnaise. Whisk to form a smooth filling.

## Fill the whites

Arrange egg whites on a serving platter and divide filling between the egg whites.

## Notes

- **Storage** — Store covered in refrigerator up to 2 days.
