# Next-Day Soft Cinnamon Rolls

## Dough

### Yudane

#### Stir

- 105 g bread flour — King Arthur
- 105 g boiling water

Stir the boiling water into the bread flour to form a paste; cool to room temperature.

### Mashed potato

#### Cook and mash

- 150 g russet potato, peeled and small-diced
- 15 g water

Microwave the potato and water in a covered microwave-safe bowl for *5 minutes*; *rest 10 minutes*, uncover, and mash. Use 104 g and discard the rest.

### Mix

- [yudane](#yudane)
- 104 g [mashed potato](#mashed-potato)
- 380 g whole milk
- 56 g egg
- 70 g sugar
- 8 g instant yeast — SAF Gold
- 595 g bread flour — King Arthur
- 1.4 g dough improver — Bakers Club

Add everything to the mixer bowl and mix on *speed 1* (Famag IM-5S) *for 2 minutes*.

### Rest

Rest *10 minutes*.

### Add salt

- 14 g salt

Mix on *speed 2 for 4 minutes*, then *speed 4 for 4 minutes*.

### Add butter

- 70 g unsalted butter, soft

Add the butter in 3–4 additions; mix on *speed 2 for 3 minutes*, then *speed 6 for 6 minutes*; *rest 10 minutes*, then *speed 6 for 2 minutes*.

### Knead and ball

The dough should be smooth, soft, and tacky. Use a few slap-and-folds on a clean countertop to bring it into a smooth ball. Place in a bowl and cover.

### Raise

Raise *60–75 minutes* *at 78–80°F* until 60–70% risen.

## Cream cheese frosting

### Beat

- 113 g unsalted butter, softened
- 113 g cream cheese, softened
- 5 g vanilla
- 1 pinch kosher salt
- 120 g powdered sugar

Beat the butter, cream cheese, vanilla, and salt. Add the powdered sugar and beat until light and smooth.

## Cinnamon goo filling

### Cook

- 150 g dark brown sugar
- 15 g tapioca starch
- 5 g Saigon cinnamon
- 5 g Korintje cinnamon
- 1.5 g kosher salt
- 135 g heavy cream
- 70 g unsalted butter

Whisk the brown sugar, tapioca starch, cinnamon, and salt; whisk in the heavy cream and add the butter. Heat *over medium*, whisking constantly, until it reaches a gentle simmer; cook *45–60 seconds* until thickened.

### Stir in the vanilla

- 4 g vanilla

Off heat, stir in the vanilla. Cool until spreadable before applying.

## Roll and fill

- [dough](#dough)
- [cinnamon goo filling](#cinnamon-goo-filling)
- flour — for dusting

On a lightly floured surface, roll the dough to roughly *18×18 inches*. The dough is soft and won't hold a tight cylinder, so handle it gently. Spread the cooled cinnamon goo evenly over the dough.

## Slice and pan

Roll up into a loose cylinder. Trim the ends if needed and slice into 12 even rolls with floss. Line a *12×16-inch pan* with parchment and arrange the rolls 3×4. Cover and let rise while the oven preheats.

## Bake

*Preheat the oven to 325°F*, rack upper-middle (no stone or steel). Bake *30–35 minutes*, rotating halfway; tent if browning early. Target *190°F internal* temperature.

## Frost

- [cream cheese frosting](#cream-cheese-frosting)

When the rolls are hot, spread half the frosting to melt it; cool *5–20 minutes*, then apply the remaining frosting.
