# Navy Bean Soup

## Beans — the night before

### Rinse and brine the beans

- 500 g navy beans — or Great Northern, Cannellini, Flageolet, or 17 bean mix
- 3/4 tbsp kosher salt
- 1000 g water

Check beans and rinse thoroughly. Brine in the salted water *overnight*.

### Drain the beans

Drain and rinse beans well.

## Soup

### Sauté the ham

- 28 g olive oil
- 400 g ham, diced

Begin by sautéing ham in olive oil over *medium heat*.

### Add the vegetables

- 170 g onion, diced
- 480 g carrots, diced
- 220 g celery stalks, sliced

While ham is cooking, dice onion and add to pot. Clean and dice carrots and add to pot. Clean and dice celery and add to pot.

### Season and sauté

- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- 1 bay leaf

Add salt, black pepper and bay leaf. Sauté *5 minutes* while cleaning and dicing potato.

### Add the garlic

- 3 cloves garlic, minced

Mince and add garlic. Cook *1 minute*.

### Add potatoes, beans, and water

- [beans](#beans--the-night-before)
- 450 g white potatoes, peeled and diced
- 2000 g water

Add potatoes, and drained beans. Add 2000 g of water.

### Boil

Slow boil for *135 minutes* *or* bring to *full pressure for 15 minutes*.

### Adjust and serve

- water — to bring the soup to 3600 grams

Add water to bring total weight of soup to 3600 grams. Serve.
