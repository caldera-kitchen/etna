# Chili with Short Ribs

## Beans — The Night Before

### Soak the beans

- 1 lb Dry beans
- 1 1/2 tbsp Kosher Salt — for the soak
- 2 quarts water — for the soak

Rinse beans and soak *overnight* in 2 quarts of cool water and 1½ tablespoons kosher salt.

## Pressure Cooker

### Beef

#### Brown the beef

- 1 1/4 lb short ribs, cut into 1½-inch pieces
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil

Salt the beef. Place oil in the deep pressure cooker and heat over *medium-high*. When the oil is hot, add the beef and DO NOT MOVE it around. There should be a lot of sizzling. After about 4 minutes, turn beef. Continue cooking in this manner *until the beef is well browned on all sides*.

#### Pressure cook the beef

- 1/2 tbsp beef bouillon
- 1 quart water

Add 1 quart of water, beef bouillon, stir well, cover, and bring to *full pressure* over high heat. Once the pressure cooker reaches full pressure, adjust temperature to maintain full pressure and cook *for 45 minutes*. Turn off heat and *let pressure drop naturally*.

#### Remove the beef

Remove the beef to a bowl and *reserve the beef stock*.

### Beans

#### Pressure cook the beans

- [beans](#beans--the-night-before)
- 1 tsp Kosher Salt — for cooking
- 6 cups water — for cooking

Drain and rinse beans well. Add beans to the now empty pressure cooker, 6 cups of water, and 1 teaspoon of kosher salt. Bring to *full pressure* over high heat, reduce heat to medium-low to maintain full pressure, and cook *for 18 minutes*. Turn off heat and *let pressure drop naturally*.

## Chili

### Cook the vegetables

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, finely diced
- 1 medium red bell pepper, finely diced
- 2 medium carrots, finely diced
- kosher salt — to taste
- 1/8 tsp black pepper

Heat oil *in Dutch oven* over *medium-high heat* until shimmering. Add onion, bell pepper, and carrots. Season with salt and pepper. Cook *covered about 10 minutes* until vegetables start to soften and begin to caramelize, stirring occasionally.

### Toast the spices

- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 2 tsp ground cumin

Sprinkle chili powder and cumin over vegetables. Stir for *1 minute* to toast.

### Stir in the tomatoes

- [beef](#beef)
- 28 oz crushed tomato
- 1/2 tbsp beef bouillon
- 1 chipotle chile in adobo sauce, seeded and minced
- 1 tsp adobo sauce from the can of chipotles
- 1/2 tsp dried oregano

Add crushed tomatoes and 1 cup of reserved beef stock. Stir in beef bouillon, chipotle, adobo sauce, and oregano.

### Add the beans and beef

- 30 oz [beans](#beans)
- [beef](#beef)

Drain beans and add 30 ounces of beans to the chili. Shred the beef and add to the chili. *Simmer for 20 minutes*.

### Thicken with masa

- 2 tbsp masa harina

Place masa in small bowl. Skim liquid off top of chili and slowly whisk into flour to form a uniform and very thin paste. Stir back into chili and *simmer until thick*.
